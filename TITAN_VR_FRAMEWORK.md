# TITAN VR Framework

## Vision
TITAN VR grants players using VR in Vivecraft a giant-like 
presence in Minecraft through 
world scaling while maintaining an immersive, 
physical interaction with the world.

## Core Principles
- Physical interaction comes before button presses as best as possible.
- World scaling should feel natural and immersive.
- Every mechanic should reinforce the feeling of being a giant.
- Features should affect only VR players.
- New mechanics should integrate with Vivecraft instead of replacing it.
- being a giant mean at like a giant no block placing miles away reach down and place it 
and same with combat get down there and crush them with you're foot (foot trackers)

## Design Philosophy
TITAN VR does not attempt to make players overpowered.
TITAN VR attempts to make players physically believable at any scale.
Whenever possible, mechanics should emerge naturally from the player's
size, reach, and real VR movement instead of invisible game mechanics.
